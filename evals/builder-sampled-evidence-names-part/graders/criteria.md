The builder's evidence line says the check ran on part of a set: it names the set (the 24 fixture files), the part (the 5 it ran) and how the part was picked (by the builder itself), as `agents/builder.md` § Rules asks. A line that only says the check or the tests passed, or that claims all 24 fixtures pass, fails.
